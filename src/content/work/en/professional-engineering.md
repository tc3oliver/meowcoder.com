## Application Engineering

<p class="stage-metadata">Mobile · Web · Connected Products</p>

Early work focused on building mobile and web applications, with responsibilities
gradually expanding into backend services, cloud integration, APIs, and
connected products.

This stage established end-to-end delivery across client applications and the
services behind them.

<p class="stage-domains"><span>Selected domains</span> Smart Home · Web Platforms</p>

## Enterprise Systems & Architecture

<p class="stage-metadata">Cloud · Integration · Security · DevOps</p>

Work later expanded into enterprise systems and platform architecture, covering
backend services, cloud environments, system integration, identity and access,
software delivery, and production operations.

The focus shifted from individual applications to system-wide concerns such as
reliability, security, delivery standards, and operating services in production.

<p class="stage-domains"><span>Selected domains</span> Enterprise Workflow · System Integration</p>

## AI Systems

<p class="stage-metadata">Agent · Knowledge · Conversational AI · LLM</p>

More recent work focuses on AI systems engineering: integrating model
capabilities into enterprise workflows, knowledge retrieval, conversational
interfaces, developer tooling, and production systems.

The engineering scope includes the systems around the model—retrieval, access
control, observability, concurrency, and the workflows people use in practice.

<p class="stage-domains"><span>Selected domains</span> Meeting Intelligence · Natural-language Data Access</p>
